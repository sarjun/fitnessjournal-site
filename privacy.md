# FitnessJournal Privacy Policy

FitnessJournal accesses Google health and fitness data only for the signed-in account owner, with their consent.

Data is downloaded to and stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked anytime at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Questions: open an issue at [github.com/sarjun/fitnessjournal-site](https://github.com/sarjun/fitnessjournal-site/issues).
